## Navigating the LLM-First DevEx Future

While LLM brings tremendous opportunities, we must acknowledge challenges and prepare for the exciting road ahead.
{: .highlight-box}

### Key Challenges:

- **Technical Risks** - Model drift, hallucinations, black box decisions
- **Skill Gap** - Need for LLM/ML expertise in DevOps teams
- **Integration Complexity** - Incorporating LLM into existing workflows
- **Cost Management** - LLM infrastructure and compute costs
- **Change Management** - Team adaptation to LLM-driven processes

### Exciting Opportunities:

- **Autonomous Operations** - Self-healing, self-optimizing systems
- **Democratized Expertise** - LLM makes advanced capabilities accessible
- **Enhanced Innovation** - Focus on creative work, not repetitive boring tasks
- **Predictive Everything** - Anticipate and prevent issues before they occur
- **Natural Language Infrastructure** - Talk to your systems in plain English
