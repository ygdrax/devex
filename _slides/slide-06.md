### LLM-Powered Development Tools:

**Copilot/Claude/OpenAI/Gemeni/Grok**
- IDE/CLI/chat
- great for code generation, bugfixing, explanation, testing, debugging, improving, generating plans and architecture decisions records.

**Copilot/CodeRabbit/Qudo**
- PR summaries, inline fixes, policy linting
- Scales reviews when humans are swamped

**Atlassian Rovo**
- Search/chat/agents over Jira/Confluence/Bitbucket
- Turns scattered knowledge into answers
