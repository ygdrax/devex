## ML and LLMs Transform Everything into DevOps V2

The breakthrough in ML and Large Language Models has enabled **DevOps V2** - intelligent, self-healing, predictive systems that actively participate in development work.
{: .highlight-box}

### Key LLM Breakthroughs:

- **Natural Language Understanding** - Communicate with systems in plain English
- **Code Generation & Review** - LLM writes, reviews, and optimizes code
- **Pattern Recognition** - Identify issues and anomalies automatically
- **Autonomous Decision Making** - LLM makes intelligent operational decisions
- **Continuous Learning** - Systems that improve over time
